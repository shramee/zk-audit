- **Primed examples.** At this revision, `shared-rules.md` (inlined into every
  bundle), `SKILL.md` and `report-format.md` used `TransferCircuit`,
  `divHint`, `res[1]`, `Pool.withdraw` and a discarded `IsZero(Recipient)` as
  examples. That primes hunters toward P2 and P7. Fixed in `15e8c19`, with a
  docs test that keeps eval identifiers out of plugin docs. Re-run before
  comparing against this baseline.
- **One run.** No variance estimate.
- **No std source.** Hunters reasoned about gnark std from memory; three
  items stayed needs_validation or lead for that reason.
